Straight answers

The questions everyone asks.

Is it really this cheap?

Yes. We run our own finance tracking, project management, and website on AI agents, which keeps our overheads low. The prices on this page are real starting prices for published work.

What if it breaks?

Every system we ship is monitored, backed up, and tested. On an AI Ops Partner plan, we watch it day to day and fix issues as they arise. When you need help, you speak directly with Waseem rather than a ticket queue.

Do I need to understand AI?

No. You know your business and where time is being lost; we handle the software and explain every step in plain English.

Will a human actually answer?

Always. Agents do the background work, while Waseem makes every decision and takes every call. When you get in touch, he reads it himself and replies personally. We promise a meeting within one week of first contact.

Where are you, and do you work remotely?

We are based in Bradford and work with businesses in Leeds, Manchester, and across the UK. Most of our work runs remotely.

What if I just want a website?

We build those too: fast, compliant websites with no monthly platform fees.
